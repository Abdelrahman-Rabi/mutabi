# Mutabi English hackathon video

The draft uses actual screen recordings from an isolated local discovery workspace, the workflow infographic from the slide deck, English narration and captions. It demonstrates a working workflow rather than a measured operator study.

## What the viewer sees

- A synthetic customer promise for SAR 3,000 on 5 October is analyzed using the curated demo provider.
- The recorded commitment produces Sara's balance-check task for 6 October.
- An employee approves the acknowledgment before delivery to the simulated inbox.
- The workspace clock advances. A fresh synthetic unpaid accounting snapshot (TC-15) is verified before the due check produces a missed promise and an approval-required follow-up.
- A separately loaded installment story receives an allocated synthetic SAR 1,000 payment: the installment is fulfilled while SAR 2,000 remains outstanding on the invoice.
- The impact calculator shows conditional estimates: 50 hours freed at the illustrative workload; net monthly cash is SAR -500 at zero cash realization and SAR 500 at 40% realization. Capacity value is not an additional cash saving.

## Production and evidence

Generic reference narration uses the installed Microsoft David Desktop English voice. It does not imitate a team member. Captions are included in the video and supplied separately as SRT. Caption timings are approximate within each narration segment.

The recorder verifies the displayed state for promise creation, task date, simulated delivery, missed promise, fulfilled installment and calculator outputs. The 120-test statement refers to the complete suite recorded in `ADVANCED_VALIDATION.md`; it is not a business impact or universal accuracy claim.

Raw screen recordings, disposable databases, synthesis files and render scripts are retained under ignored `runtime/video/`. The main product and existing workspaces are not reset for the recording. Only the separate recording session is prepared.
