# Mutabi hackathon video — English narration

Target: 2–3 minutes. Actual local UI footage, workflow infographic, English reference narration and subtitles. This is a product demonstration, not a timed operator study. Synthetic data, curated interpretation and simulated accounting/delivery remain labeled. Reference voice: Microsoft David Desktop, a generic installed English voice.

## 1. Turn a payment promise into accountable work

For a small distributor selling on credit, a payment promise creates more work: record the details, remember the date, check accounting, and choose the next contact. Mutabi brings those steps into one accountable workflow.

Visual: Branded opening: customer promise, recorded commitment, verified next action.

## 2. A complete payment-promise workflow

The workflow links customer replies, employee review, owned commitments, and accounting evidence. Paid, partial, missed, and unverified payments lead to different actions. Employees stay in control of customer contact.

Visual: Generated workflow infographic. Synthetic prototype; checks run on demand.

## 3. 1 · Interpret and review the promise

This synthetic customer promises three thousand riyals on October fifth. We analyze the reply and inspect its amount and date. This is a curated demo example; free text requires manual review, with live AI interpretation available separately.

Visual: Fresh INV-001 conversation. Click Analyze reply, then show its recorded commitment. Label Recorded demo example.

## 4. 2 · Give the next check an owner and date

Mutabi records the commitment and creates a dated balance-check task, assigned to Sara. The promise and next action stay linked to the invoice. The balance remains unchanged, because a promise is not a payment.

Visual: Promises tab, then Tasks tab. Show amount/date and Sara's 6 October check.

## 5. 3 · Review, approve, then deliver

The acknowledgment is a draft. An employee reviews and approves it. Delivery is a separate action, guarded by approval and accounting evidence. Here we deliver to the simulated inbox, without changing the invoice balance.

Visual: Review acknowledgment, Approve, Send to simulated inbox. Show delivery proof.

## 6. 4 · Let accounting evidence decide what happens next

We move the demo clock to October sixth and run the due check. No verified payment has arrived. Mutabi marks the promise missed and prepares a follow-up for approval, while keeping the unpaid balance visible. Checks run on demand.

Visual: Demo studio clock change, verify the fresh TC-15 synthetic unpaid accounting snapshot, return to the same account, Run due follow-ups, missed promise and new draft.

## 7. An installment fulfilled is not an invoice settled

A separate installment example shows an important distinction. A verified synthetic payment of one thousand riyals fulfills that commitment. The invoice still has two thousand outstanding. Mutabi tracks the installment and remaining debt separately.

Visual: Explicitly label Separate synthetic case. Load installment story, simulate/allocate SAR1000, show fulfilled promise and SAR2000 balance.

## 8. Let each business test its own assumptions

We have not measured business savings yet. Businesses can test their own workload and costs in the calculator. These illustrative inputs project fifty hours freed monthly. That is capacity, not automatically cash. At an assumed forty percent spending reduction, net monthly cash is five hundred riyals. At zero percent, it is negative five hundred.

Visual: Calculator default inputs and results; show zero realization, then enter40. Persistent label Scenario projection—not measured impact.

## 9. Working prototype. Clear assumptions. A measurable next step.

The prototype passed one hundred and twenty automated tests on tested behavior. Accounting and delivery are simulated. Next, an operator pilot will measure time to a correct action, actual spending changes, and follow-up quality. Mutabi makes every promise accountable.
